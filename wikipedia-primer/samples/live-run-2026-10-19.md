## carbon credits: a primer

Carbon credits are tradable instruments representing a reduction or removal of one metric ton of greenhouse gases, used to offset emissions or comply with regulations (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verified_Carbon_Standard). They work through projects like reforestation or renewable energy that generate these credits, which can then be bought and sold in voluntary or compliance markets (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verified_Carbon_Standard). Criticisms include concerns over the effectiveness and integrity of some projects, especially forestry credits, with investigations revealing that many certified offsets may be worthless or even worsen climate change (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verified_Carbon_Standard). The standards governing these credits are diverse, and issues of fraud and lack of transparency have been reported (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_offsets_and_credits).

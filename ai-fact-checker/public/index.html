AI Fact Checker

Made by Desearch | https://desearch.ai

Paste one claim. The app searches the web (and optionally Wikipedia) with the Desearch API, shows the sources it found with a link to each, and a short summary that hides judgment language and keeps only lines tied to a returned source. It helps you find sources; it does not decide what is true.

A check takes about 10 to 15 seconds and costs $0.004 ($0.008 with Wikipedia). Cached checks cost nothing. Optional X search adds $0.0015 and can take up to 40 seconds.

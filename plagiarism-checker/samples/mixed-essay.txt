Notes from a coastal allotment

My small allotment sits three streets back from the harbour, and the salt wind flattens the broad beans every March. A lighthouse is a tower, building, or other type of physical structure designed to emit light from a system of lamps and lenses and to serve as a beacon for navigational aid for maritime pilots at sea or on inland waterways. Tides are the regular rise and fall of the sea level, caused by the gravitational pull of the Moon and the Sun together with the rotation of the Earth. Last spring I counted forty-one snails on a single cabbage after a night of drizzle, which convinced me to try copper tape around the raised beds. When we look to the individuals of the same variety or sub-variety of our older cultivated plants and animals, one of the first points which strikes us, is, that they generally differ much more from each other, than do the individuals of any one species or variety in a state of nature. The old keeper's cottage near the pier now sells second-hand gardening books, and I found a faded guide to growing leeks in sandy soil there for two pounds. Whether or not the copper tape works, I now plant marigolds along the edge of every bed because they make the plot look cared for.

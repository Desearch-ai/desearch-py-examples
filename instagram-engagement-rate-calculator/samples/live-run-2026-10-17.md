**Formula.** Per post: engagement rate % = (likes + comments) / followers x 100. Account rate = the mean of the post rates. The median is shown next to it.

Based on the latest posts Desearch returned for each account. Left out of the rate: likely pinned posts (listed first but older), posts under 24 hours old, and posts with hidden likes. Saves and shares are not in the data, so this is a likes plus comments rate. Average views are shown only for posts where Desearch returned a view count.
